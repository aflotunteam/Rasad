==================================================
1. PRODUCT DEFINITION
==================================================

RASAD is an intelligent economic-risk analysis and decision-support platform.

Its purpose is to:

1. combine economic data from multiple authorized sources;
2. validate and assess data quality;
3. identify unusual patterns, deviations and risk signals;
4. calculate a configurable analytical risk score;
5. show the confidence of that result;
6. explain the main factors behind the score;
7. analyse time dynamics, peer deviations and relationships;
8. prioritise cases requiring human attention;
9. allow an authorised expert to review the result;
10. record the expert’s final operational decision.

CORE PRINCIPLE:

RASAD does NOT determine guilt.
RASAD does NOT automatically declare a business illegal.
RASAD does NOT make a final legal decision.

RASAD answers:

“Qaysi holat qo‘shimcha ekspert tahlilini birinchi navbatda talab qiladi va nima sababdan?”

The final decision remains with an authorised human expert.
